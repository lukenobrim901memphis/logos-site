Website Origins

As a little kid, I wanted to design everything. Maps, flags, street signs, baseball cards are just a few of the things I can remember. In 2021, I was inspired and started making templates of uniforms to keep track of baseball jersey number updates. When I got my first computer in 2023 before college, I was excited to be able to start doing some graphic designs and logos. College is where I really started making my own logos and designs. Adobe Illustrator is almost always open on my computer.

My roommate from freshman year Sebastian told me that he would come home some days and find me cowered in the dark over my computer screen working on a St. Louis Cardinals’ City Connect logo. “I would ask him when he last ate and all he would say is, ‘Huh?’”

I’ve had a vision of making a website where I can put all of the lists, logos, and jerseys that I make but never took the time to learn how to code. I’ve done a few false starts but got frustrated and gave up. This is far and away the best attempt I’ve made at turning that vision into something.

Who Is This Luke Guy?

I grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area at the perfect time to be a Giants fan and still am one to this day. I was inspired to make this site by expanding upon sports-logos.net and their fantastic treasure trove as well as uniform-lineup.com. Currently, I’m a college student studying Journalism at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo. This site was actually birthed out of a class I took that had us learning about HTML, JavaScript, and CSS. It was the perfect boost to get me past that finish line that I’ve been trying to cross for ages.

What’s The Big Deal With Logos?

Logos and uniforms play such a big role in watching a sporting event. They are the entire fabric (haha) of a team yet they go completely under the radar. The pretty colors look a lot better than if one team wore all white and the other wore all gray. Jerry Seinfeld once said that sports fans are, “rooting for their team’s clothes to beat the clothes from another city.” Well if someone is going to keep track of the clothes, I want to do it. It requires a lot of observing to be able to accurately track all of the information.

The Hardest Logo To Do

The Toronto Blue Jays’ City Connect jersey logo was such a pain to make. I was in the dining hall for four hours until my computer died then until past midnight when I got back to my room to charge it. Another tough one was the on-field Super Bowl logo in 2026. The amount of small gradients and intricate reflections on the trophy took so much time that it took me the entire game to finish it.
